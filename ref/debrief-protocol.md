# Post-Meeting Debrief Prompt

Copy everything below the line and paste it into a new message in this project after any meeting. Drop your Otter transcript, notes, or voice memo text below the prompt.

---

## MEETING DEBRIEF — EXTRACT AND UPDATE

**Meeting with:** [name(s)]
**Date:** [today's date]
**Topic:** [one-line summary]

[PASTE OTTER TRANSCRIPT OR NOTES HERE]

---

**Run the full debrief protocol on the above. Here's what I need:**

### 1. TASK EXTRACTION
- Extract every action item that is MY responsibility
- For each: title, description, priority (High/Medium/Low), suggested due date, and who else is involved
- Flag anything that sounds like a task but is really just context or conversation (mark as FYI)
- Push all real tasks to D1 (eaton-ehs-dashboard) assigned to Charlie Ball
- Push FYI items to D1 as undated with fyi-context tag

### 2. PEOPLE INTEL UPDATE
- Any new people mentioned? Add to D1 people table with department, role, and whatever context was given
- Any updated intel on existing people? (reliability change, new strength/weakness info, political dynamics, who they're aligned with, management style observations) — update their D1 records
- Call out anyone I should be building a relationship with based on what was discussed

### 3. METRICS & GOALS
- Were any numbers, KPIs, targets, or deadlines mentioned? Capture them
- Do any conflict with or update what's already tracked in the EHS Metrics tab?
- If a goal was achieved or a metric changed, flag it for My Five or TalentHub update

### 4. PROJECT MEMORY UPDATE
- Update project memory with any significant new operational context
- Remove or correct anything that's now outdated

### 5. STRATEGIC DEBRIEF — Think like my executive coach
Ask yourself these questions about the meeting and give me honest answers:
- **What did I just learn that I don't fully appreciate yet?** (Hidden risks, political dynamics, resource opportunities, timing windows)
- **Who gained or lost power/influence in this conversation?** (Org dynamics, alignment shifts, someone being set up or sidelined)
- **What problem was discussed that nobody actually owns yet?** (Gaps I could step into)
- **What's the highest-value thing I could do in the next 48 hours based on this meeting?** (Not the most urgent — the most valuable)
- **What question should I have asked but didn't?** (Blind spots based on what was shared)
- **Is there a "citation log" moment here?** (A data nugget I should be able to repeat in a 1-on-1 or performance review — frame it in Eaton's high-performer language)

### 6. FLOOR ACTION ITEMS
Based on what was discussed, give me 2-3 specific things to go look at, verify, or talk to someone about on the plant floor. Include who to talk to and what to ask.

### FORMAT
- Lead with the floor items (I might be walking out right after reading this)
- Then tasks, people, metrics
- Strategic debrief last (I'll read this when I sit down)
- Keep it tight — I'm reading this on my phone between meetings
