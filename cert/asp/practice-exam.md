# Practice Exam — 100 Questions

Blueprint-weighted to ASP11. Grouped by domain so you can drill a single area, but the real
exam shuffles them — for a realistic run, work straight through, timed.

**Timing:** 150 minutes for 100 questions (the real exam's 90-second-per-question pace).
**Rules:** closed book, TI-30XS only, no notes, one sitting.

Worked solutions are in [`practice-exam-answers.md`](practice-exam-answers.md). Score
yourself by domain before you look at anything.

## Domain 1 — Mathematical Calculations

**1.** A facility worked 1,450,000 hours last year and recorded 18 OSHA-recordable cases.
Seven involved days away from work and five involved job transfer or restriction. What is
the DART rate?
A. 0.97 B. 1.66 C. 2.48 D. 2.90

**2.** An employee is exposed to 98 dBA for 2 hours and 88 dBA for 6 hours. Using OSHA
criteria, what is the noise dose?
A. 87% B. 107% C. 133% D. 157%

**3.** A 175-lb worker falls 6 feet before a shock-absorbing lanyard begins to deploy and
brings him to a stop over 3.0 feet. What is the approximate arresting force?
A. 350 lbf B. 525 lbf C. 613 lbf D. 1,050 lbf

**4.** A two-leg bridle sling supports a 9,000-lb load with the legs at 30° from horizontal.
What is the tension in each leg?
A. 4,500 lb B. 5,196 lb C. 6,364 lb D. 9,000 lb

**5.** A hood with a 3 ft² face opening must produce a capture velocity of 100 fpm at a
distance of 12 inches. Assuming an unflanged plain opening, what airflow is required?
A. 300 cfm B. 1,000 cfm C. 1,300 cfm D. 3,300 cfm

**6.** Xylene has a molecular weight of 106. A sample result of 87 mg/m³ corresponds to what
concentration in ppm at NTP?
A. 8.6 ppm B. 20.1 ppm C. 43.5 ppm D. 377 ppm

**7.** A radiation source produces 480 mR/hr at 2 feet. At what distance will the dose rate
be 30 mR/hr?
A. 4 ft B. 8 ft C. 16 ft D. 32 ft

**8.** A control system has three components in series with reliabilities of 0.97, 0.94, and
0.99. What is the system reliability?
A. 0.90 B. 0.9027 C. 0.9670 D. 0.9982

**9.** A company is evaluating a $48,000 guarding project expected to avoid $18,000 per year
in losses. What is the simple payback period?
A. 1.6 years B. 2.7 years C. 3.75 years D. 0.375 years

**10.** A worker is exposed to 180 ppm for 2 hours, 90 ppm for 3 hours, and no exposure for
the remaining 3 hours of an 8-hour shift. What is the 8-hour TWA?
A. 78.8 ppm B. 90.0 ppm C. 126.0 ppm D. 135.0 ppm

## Domain 2 — Safety Programs and Concepts

**11.** Which of the following represents the correct order of the ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 hierarchy

**16.** In a lockout/tagout procedure, which step must be performed last before servicing

is the current, and what is the likely physiological effect?

**19.** A ground fault circuit interrupter primarily protects:
A. Conductors from overload
B. Equipment from short circuit

**20.** Under NFPA 70E, the arc flash boundary is the distance at which incident energy
equals:
A. 1.2 cal/cm² B. 8 cal/cm² C. 40 cal/cm² D. 50 volts

**21.** A location where flammable gases may be present only under abnormal conditions, such
as a container failure, is classified as:
A. Class I, Division 1 B. Class I, Division 2 C. Class II, Division 1 D. Class III, Division 2

**22.** OSHA requires a protective system in an excavation at what depth, and a professional
engineer's design at what depth?
A. 4 ft and 15 ft B. 5 ft and 20 ft C. 6 ft and 25 ft D. 5 ft and 25 ft

**23.** A guardrail top rail must withstand a force of at least:
A. 100 lbf B. 150 lbf C. 200 lbf D. 500 lbf

**24.** For a bench grinder, the maximum permitted gap between the work rest and the wheel,
and between the tongue guard and the wheel, are respectively:
A. 1/4 in and 1/8 in B. 1/8 in and 1/4 in C. 1/16 in and 1/8 in D. 1/2 in and 1/4 in

**25.** A forklift rated 5,500 lb at a 24-inch load center is used with a load whose center
of gravity is 36 inches from the face of the forks. The approximate capacity is:
A. 2,750 lb B. 3,667 lb C. 4,125 lb D. 8,250 lb

**26.** How often must a powered industrial truck operator's performance be evaluated?
A. Annually B. Every two years C. At least every three years D. Only after an incident

**27.** Under PSM, how often must a process hazard analysis be updated and revalidated?
A. Annually B. Every 3 years C. Every 5 years D. Only when a change occurs

**28.** Under PSM, an incident investigation must be initiated within:
A. 24 hours B. 48 hours C. 72 hours D. 5 days

**29.** Which of the following is an example of an engineering control?
A. A written procedure requiring hearing protection
B. Rotating workers out of a high-noise area every two hours
C. Enclosing a noisy compressor in an acoustic housing
D. Posting a "hearing protection required" sign

**30.** A section 8 of a safety data sheet contains:
A. First-aid measures
B. Exposure controls and personal protection
C. Toxicological information
D. Stability and reactivity

**31.** Under GHS, which is more severe?
A. Category 4 B. Category 1 C. They are equivalent D. Depends on the pictogram

**32.** An investigator concludes that an incident occurred because "the operator failed to
follow the lockout procedure." This conclusion is best described as:
A. A root cause
B. A surface cause requiring further analysis
C. A contributing factor that fully explains the event

**33.** Which of the following best distinguishes a leading indicator from a lagging
indicator?
A. Leading indicators are always quantitative
B. Leading indicators measure activities that predict future performance
C. Leading indicators are required by OSHA
D. Leading indicators are only useful at large facilities

**34.** A temporary piping modification is installed to keep production running during a
repair. Under management of change, what element most directly addresses the risk that this
becomes permanent?
A. Technical basis for the change
B. Authorization requirements
C. A defined time period for the change
D. Training of affected employees

**35.** Which condition would make a space permit-required even if the atmosphere is

A. It has a single point of entry
